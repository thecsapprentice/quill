The lighthouse keeper's last letter

Open full page
Marrow (@marrow)TeenOpenOct 26, 2026

A letter arrives forty years late, addressed to a keeper who never left the rock. The envelope is dry. The ink is still wet.

I'd like to write the village's side: the postmistress who found it, the families who remember the wreck. You'd write the keeper, answering in letters that keep arriving out of order.

Slow pace, a few hundred words a turn. Happy to go dark but not gory.
